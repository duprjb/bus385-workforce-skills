### Proficiency Methodology

Our survey measures respondents' proficiency from 1 to 5.

The initial job-role proficiency requirements were created as illustrative values to test our PostgreSQL database.

O*NET job-posting percentages establish the relevance of technologies but do not specify exact proficiency levels. We will document separate proficiency criteria for each technical skill and distinguish project-assigned requirements from official O*NET measurements.

For O*NET skills with applicable level scores, we will document the original measure, its scale, and the conversion methodology before finalizing requirements.
